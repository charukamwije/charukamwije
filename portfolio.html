02 Method

  1. parameteriseExpose the open design decisions as sliders, domains and toggles.
  2. constrainEncode performance, fabrication and site limits as rules the geometry must meet.
  3. generateRun the definition across the parameter space and keep the outputs that pass.
  4. selectFilter results with multi-objective search, then review them by eye.
  5. buildExport fabrication-ready geometry with panel IDs, tolerances and nesting.
