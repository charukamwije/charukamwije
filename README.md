### 🌱 Featured project: [Aswedduma](https://sl-grow.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/charukamwije/sl-grow)

A production-style web app that helps Sri Lankan households grow vegetables at home.

- **Planner:** turns a family's diet, space and market prices into a grow plan, a shopping list and a payback estimate
- **E-commerce:** a shop of about 50 products with a cart that merges across devices, Google sign-in, cash-on-delivery or bank-transfer checkout, and order tracking
- **Plantation ROI tool:** NPV, IRR, break-even price and payback for cinnamon and tea, with every assumption editable
- **Three languages:** English, සිංහල and தமிழ்
- **Engineering:** Next.js and TypeScript, Supabase (auth, Postgres, migrations), automated tests, GitHub Actions CI, deployed on Vercel
